Printing Instructions

Everything prints on ordinary Letter (8½×11″) paper — at home or at any print shop.

The golden rules

  1. Always print a test copy on plain paper first. Check names, dates, and which way the back side comes out — then load the good paper.
  2. In the print window, set margins to None (or Minimum) and turn backgrounds ON (Chrome/Edge: More settings → “Background graphics”; Safari: check “Print backgrounds”). This keeps the soft colors and borders.
  3. Set Scale to 100% (not “Fit to page”).
  4. To make a PDF instead of paper: press Print, then choose “Save as PDF” as the destination. That PDF is exactly what a print shop needs.

Any office store sells 65 lb cardstock — one 50-sheet pack usually covers a whole service.

4-page program (1 sheet, both sides)

  1. Open the Studio → choose Funeral Program — 4 pages → press Print.
  2. If your printer prints double-sided: choose “Print on both sides — flip on short edge.” Done.
  3. If not: print page 1 only, put the sheet back in the tray (printed side per your printer's habit — this is what the plain-paper test is for), then print page 2.
  4. Fold down the middle. The cover lands on the front, the order of service and obituary inside.
Back side upside-down? Your printer flips the other way — reprint the second side choosing “flip on long edge” instead. This varies by printer; the test sheet tells you in 60 seconds.

8-page program (2 sheets, both sides)

  1. Choose Funeral Program — 8 pages → Print → double-sided, flip on short edge.
  2. The sheet showing the front cover (their name and photo) is the outside sheet.
  3. Nest the other sheet inside it, fold both together down the middle — the pages fall in perfect order (the Studio arranges the page pairs for you).
  4. Optional: two staples on the fold (any long-arm stapler, or ask the print shop).

Prayer cards & invitations (cutting)

  • Prayer cards: print sheet 1 (fronts) and sheet 2 (backs) double-sided, flip on long edge, on cardstock. Cut along the dashed lines — six cards per sheet.
  • Invitations: two per sheet; one straight cut across the middle, then trim the edges. Fits A7 envelopes (5¼×7¼″), sold at any office or craft store.
  • A paper trimmer (or the print shop's cutter, usually about a dollar per cut) gives cleaner edges than scissors.

Using a print shop (FedEx Office, Staples, UPS Store, local printer)

  1. In the Studio, press Print → destination “Save as PDF.” Do this for each document you need.
  2. Email the PDFs to the shop or upload on their website (all three chains accept online orders with same-day pickup).
  3. Say: “Letter size, double-sided, flip on short edge, on 65 lb cardstock, and please fold and staple.” They do this daily.
  4. Welcome sign: ask for the PDF printed at 18×24″ (or 24×36″) — it scales perfectly. Most shops rent or include an easel; posterboard mounting is a few dollars more.
  5. Photo collage: can also be enlarged to poster size for the memory table.

Printing from a phone or tablet

  • iPhone/iPad: press Print in the Studio, then in the print screen pinch open the preview and use the Share icon → save/email the PDF (or AirPrint straight to a home printer).
  • Android: press Print → choose “Save as PDF” → share the PDF anywhere.
  • No printer at home? Save the PDF and send it to a print shop or a family member straight from your phone.
