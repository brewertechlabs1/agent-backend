Quick-Start Guide

From download to a printed program — five simple steps, no experience needed.

1

Open the Studio

In this folder, double-click (or tap) Celebration-of-Life-Studio.html. It opens in your web browser like a normal webpage — but it runs entirely on your device and works without internet. Not sure what it will look like filled in? Press “See an Example” at the top.

2

Pick a theme & document

On the left, choose the theme that feels right — Christian, Military Veteran, Mother/Grandmother, Father/Grandfather, Church Funeral, Red Cardinal, or Beach/Nature. Then pick what to create first (most families start with the 4-page program). You can switch themes and documents at any time — your typing carries over to every document automatically.

3

Fill in their details

Work down the numbered sections: name and dates, photos, service details, order of service, obituary. Every box is optional — anything you leave blank simply won't appear. For the obituary, open Obituary Writing Prompts in this folder — it walks you through it gently, question by question.

4

Print — or save as a PDF

Press 🖨 Print / Save as PDF. In the print window, you can send it straight to your printer or choose “Save as PDF” as the destination to get a file you can email, text, or take to a print shop. Full paper-and-settings help is in Printing Instructions.

5

Create the rest in minutes

Because your details are already typed in, the invitation, welcome sign, prayer cards, photo collage, social-media announcement, and slideshow are each just two clicks away: pick the document, then print or download. The slideshow plays full-screen — connect your device to the venue's TV or projector and press start.

💾 Your work is safe. Everything you type is saved automatically on this device. To keep a copy — or move to another device — press Save Backup (it downloads a small file), then use Load Backup on the other device.
📱 On a phone? Use the Edit Details / Preview tabs at the top to switch between typing and seeing the result. For printing from a phone, choose “Save as PDF” and share the PDF to a printer, a family member, or a print shop.
🆘 If something looks off: make sure you're opening the file in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox). If a preview looks squeezed, try making the browser window larger, or press Ctrl+0 (Cmd+0 on Mac) to reset zoom.
