## 1 · What the product is (your elevator pitch)

An **interactive, works-offline funeral template studio**: one HTML file the buyer opens
in any browser (computer, tablet, or phone — no Canva, no Word, no fonts to install,
no account). They type the details **once** and instantly get all 8 documents in any of
**7 themes**, printed at home or saved as PDFs for a print shop.

This is a genuine differentiator against Canva/Word/PDF template listings:
- **Type once → every document fills itself** (competitors make buyers re-edit each file)
- **Works on a phone** (huge — grieving buyers are often not at a computer)
- **100% private** — photos and names never leave the buyer's device
- **Auto-fitting text** — long obituaries shrink to fit; no broken layouts
- Built-in **slideshow player** for the service — no PowerPoint needed
