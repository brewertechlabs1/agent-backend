🚀 LeadFlow Pro — Quick Start

Your automated lead machine: captures leads, replies instantly, follows up automatically, books appointments, and alerts you the moment a lead is ready. Everything runs on your own computer — no monthly fees, no cloud account, your customer data never leaves your machine.

Step 1 — Install Node.js (one time, 2 minutes, free)

LeadFlow Pro runs on Node.js, a free program from nodejs.org/en/download.

1
Go to nodejs.org/en/download and download the installer for Windows or Mac.
2
Run it and click Next → Next → Finish (all default options are fine).

Already have Node.js? Skip to Step 2.

Step 2 — Start LeadFlow Pro

Windows: double-click Start-LeadFlow-WINDOWS.bat

Mac: right-click Start-LeadFlow-MAC.command and choose Open (only needed the first time — after that, double-click works).

A small black window opens (that's the engine — keep it open) and your browser opens the dashboard automatically at http://localhost:4321.

💡 If the browser doesn't open by itself, open it manually and go to http://localhost:4321

Step 3 — The 60-second setup wizard

Enter your business name, your email (where lead alerts go), and create a dashboard password. That's it — the system is live and can already capture leads.

Step 4 — Connect your email (recommended, ~2 minutes)

Until you connect an email account, LeadFlow runs in demo mode: outgoing emails are saved into the data/outbox folder instead of being sent — great for testing safely.

To send for real, go to Settings → Email Sending in the dashboard. Any normal email account works:

Step 5 — Start collecting leads

Open Help & Share in the dashboard. You'll find:

  • Your lead capture form link — put it in your Google Business profile, Facebook page, email signature, QR code on your counter, anywhere.
  • Your booking page link — customers pick a time, you both get confirmations + calendar invites + automatic reminders.
  • Embed code — paste one line into your website to show the form there.

What happens automatically

  • ⚡ Instant response: every lead gets a friendly reply within seconds — with your booking link.
  • 📨 Follow-up sequence: no booking? They're followed up at 1 day, 3 days and 7 days (fully editable in Settings).
  • 🛑 Smart stop: emails stop the moment a lead books, or you mark them won or lost.
  • 🔔 Owner alerts: you get an email the instant a lead arrives or books — call them while they're hot.
  • 📅 Reminders: booked customers get an automatic reminder 24 hours before their appointment.
  • ♻️ Old-lead revival: import your old customer list (CSV) on the Campaigns page and launch a re-engagement sequence any time.

Keeping it running

Automation runs while the app window is open. For a set-and-forget setup, make it start with your computer:

Windows: press Win + R, type shell:startup, press Enter, and copy a shortcut to Start-LeadFlow-WINDOWS.bat into that folder.

Mac: System Settings → General → Login Items → “+” → choose Start-LeadFlow-MAC.command.

Backups & moving computers

All your leads, appointments and settings live in the data folder inside the LeadFlow folder. Copy that folder to a USB stick or cloud drive to back up; copy it back to restore. To move to a new computer, move the whole LeadFlow folder.

Troubleshooting

“Node.js is not installed” — do Step 1, then start again.

Page won't load — make sure the black app window is still open; go to http://localhost:4321.

Port already in use — another program uses port 4321. Start from a terminal with a different port: PORT=4500 node app/server.js (Mac) or set PORT=4500 && node app\server.js (Windows).

Test email fails — re-check server, port, username, and use an app password (not your regular password) for Gmail/Yahoo.

Emails land in spam — send from a real mailbox you own (Gmail/Outlook), keep the “From” email identical to the account you log in with.

Forgot your dashboard password — close the app, open data/settings.json in Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac), delete the whole line that starts with "passwordHash", save the file, and start the app again. It will ask you to set a new password. (This requires access to the computer itself, so strangers can't do it remotely.)
