 LeadFlow Pro — Automated Lead Capture & Follow-Up System
==========================================================

Thank you for your purchase!

FASTEST START
  1. Install Node.js (free, one time): https://nodejs.org/en/download
  2. Windows: double-click  Start-LeadFlow-WINDOWS.bat
     Mac:     right-click   Start-LeadFlow-MAC.command  -> Open
     Linux:   run           ./start-leadflow-linux.sh
  3. Your browser opens the dashboard. Follow the 60-second wizard. Done.

FULL INSTRUCTIONS
  Open "START-HERE-Quick-Start-Guide.html" in your browser.

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  START-HERE-Quick-Start-Guide.html ... setup guide (open this first)
  Start-LeadFlow-WINDOWS.bat .......... Windows launcher
  Start-LeadFlow-MAC.command .......... Mac launcher
  start-leadflow-linux.sh ............. Linux launcher
  app/ ................................ the application (don't edit)
  data/ ............................... created on first run; ALL your leads,
                                        appointments & settings live here.
                                        Back up this folder regularly.
  LICENSE.txt ......................... your license

WHAT IT DOES, AUTOMATICALLY
  * Captures leads from your shareable form (or your website)
  * Replies to every lead instantly with your booking link
  * Follows up at 1 / 3 / 7 days (editable) until they book
  * Lets customers self-book appointments; sends confirmations,
    calendar invites, and 24-hour reminders
  * Emails YOU the moment a lead arrives or books
  * Revives old leads: import a CSV, launch a re-engagement campaign

No monthly fees. No cloud account. Your data stays on your computer.
